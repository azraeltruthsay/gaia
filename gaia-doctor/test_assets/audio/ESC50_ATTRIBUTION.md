# ESC-50 clips

The `esc_*.wav` files are from the ESC-50 dataset:

K. J. Piczak. *ESC: Dataset for Environmental Sound Classification.*
Proceedings of the 23rd Annual ACM Conference on Multimedia, 2015.
https://github.com/karolpiczak/ESC-50 — https://doi.org/10.7910/DVN/YDEPUT

Licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0 (CC BY-NC 3.0),
from freesound.org recordings. Used here unmodified except resampled from
44.1 kHz to 16 kHz mono and renamed by category (one fold-1 clip per category).

The `podcast_e13_*.wav` clips are the first 40 s of GAIA podcast episode 13
(Azrael's own NotebookLM deep dive), split into two 20 s clips.
